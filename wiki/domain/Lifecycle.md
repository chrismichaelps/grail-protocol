# Lifecycle

- **Definition:** Where a task stands: submitted, working, input-required, or one of five final states.
- **Canonical name:** Lifecycle (never status or job).
- **Not:** Delivery acknowledgement, which is the cursor's job.
- **Example:** Task `t1` moved from submitted to working to completed.
