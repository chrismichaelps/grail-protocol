# Participant

- **Definition:** Anything that sends or receives envelopes: an agent, a tool, a memory store, a person, a service, or a graph.
- **Canonical name:** Participant (never node, actor, or client).
- **Not:** A process; one process may host many participants.
- **Example:** `writer`, an agent with role `author` and ability `draft`.
