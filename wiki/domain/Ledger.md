# Ledger

- **Definition:** The append-only, hash-linked record of every accepted envelope of a scope.
- **Canonical name:** Ledger (never log or journal).
- **Not:** The server log, which records operations rather than envelopes.
- **Example:** Entry 3: `r1`, its digest, and the link after entry 2.
