# Edge

- **Definition:** A directed link that either authorizes flow (`flow`) or declares that work waits on another participant (`dependency`).
- **Canonical name:** Edge, with relation flow or dependency.
- **Not:** A causal parent, which links envelopes rather than participants.
- **Example:** `d1`: writer to reviewer, dependency.
