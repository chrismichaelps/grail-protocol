# Problem

- **Definition:** A refusal: a registered code, its status and title from the contract, a detail for people, and extra members.
- **Canonical name:** Problem (never error object).
- **Not:** A lifecycle error, which is an envelope of kind `error`.
- **Example:** `grail.dependency-unmet` with `unmet: [writer]`.
