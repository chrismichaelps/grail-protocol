# Mutation

- **Definition:** An atomic, revision-checked list of graph changes.
- **Canonical name:** Mutation.
- **Not:** A graph creation, which is recorded as a mutation from revision 0.
- **Example:** Revision 1 to 2: add edge `d2`.
