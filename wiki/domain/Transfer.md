# Transfer

- **Definition:** One envelope moving along edges, from acceptance to delivery, with its lifecycle when it has one.
- **Canonical name:** Transfer; its id is the envelope's id.
- **Not:** A network request.
- **Example:** Transfer `t1`, delivered to `team/writer`, state `working`.
