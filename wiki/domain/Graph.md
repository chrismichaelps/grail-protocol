# Graph

- **Definition:** A versioned set of participants and the edges between them, inside one scope.
- **Canonical name:** Graph (never workflow or pipeline).
- **Not:** The ledger's causal graph, which is made of envelopes, not participants.
- **Example:** Graph `team` at revision 3.
