# Scope

- **Definition:** The isolation boundary owned by one principal; every other object lives inside exactly one scope.
- **Canonical name:** Scope (never tenant, workspace, or account).
- **Not:** An organization's directory of users: who owns a scope is outside the protocol.
- **Example:** Scope `demo` holds graph `team` and a ledger of 4 entries.
